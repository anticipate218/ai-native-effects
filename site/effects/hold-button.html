Hold to Confirm

A replacement for "Are you sure?" dialogs on irreversible actions. Press and hold: a liquid fill with a rippling front advances while the button squishes slightly and its glow builds, with a little shake near the end. Release early and it drains back; hold to the end for a check mark, a burst and an optional haptic tap. Mouse, touch and keyboard (hold Space or Enter).
