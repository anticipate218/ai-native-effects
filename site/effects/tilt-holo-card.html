Holographic Tilt Card

A card that tilts towards the pointer on springs. A procedural foil (glitter, grooves or scales) shows a rainbow only where the light hits, a broad iridescent band and a glare follow the pointer, and the rim shimmers. Your own HTML goes inside. When nobody touches it, it sways gently; on phones it can follow the gyroscope.

Move the pointer over the card, drag on touch, or Tab to the card's link.
