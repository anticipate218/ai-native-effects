Satellite Scan Reveal

Two waits in every remote-sensing product, made legible. A push-broom sensor line reveals the scene (band fringes and detector striping calibrate away behind it), then a dashed inference sweep lays the model's land-cover mask over it. Bring your own image and mask, or let it generate a farmland scene.
