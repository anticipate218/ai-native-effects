Decrypt Text

Headlines, labels and numbers that decode themselves. Unlike most scramble effects, the line never jitters: every character cell keeps its final width and scrambles only through glyphs of a similar width. Change the text and it morphs from the old string to the new one; Chinese and Japanese scramble through their own glyphs.
