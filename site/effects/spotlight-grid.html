Spotlight Grid

One shared light for a whole wall of cards. It follows the pointer with a little inertia, lights the card borders (neighbours too, right across the gaps), reveals a dot grid behind them and sends a light wave through the wall when you click. Your cards stay your own markup; style the element as your grid.

Everything you need
to ship, in one place.
