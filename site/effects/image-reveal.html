Image Reveal FX

Image models make people wait behind a spinner. This component shows the generation the way a diffusion sampler actually works: noise is removed step by step, the picture emerges coarse to fine out of latent blocks, and a step counter keeps pace with your backend. When it is done, drag the divider to compare the starting noise with the result.
