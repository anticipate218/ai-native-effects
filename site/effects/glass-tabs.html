Glass Tabs

A tab bar whose selected tab sits under a liquid-glass lens. The lens really refracts and magnifies the icons and labels beneath it, with a touch of chromatic dispersion; switching tabs, it slides on a spring and stretches with its speed like a drop of liquid. Drag the lens to scrub between tabs. Your tabs stay your own buttons.
