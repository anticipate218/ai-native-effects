Liquid Glass Lens

A liquid-glass lens that refracts the live content it wraps (text stays selectable). In read mode it shows an assistant reading a source: the lens glides along the lines, magnifies what it reads, pauses on the passages it cites and leaves them highlighted. Switch to follow and move your pointer over the page.
