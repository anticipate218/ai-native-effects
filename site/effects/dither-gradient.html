Dither Gradient

A flowing gradient, or any image, rendered through a limited palette with ordered (Bayer) or noise dithering: print, risograph or an old handheld screen. Move the pointer: a lens shows the smooth, undithered colours underneath. Put your own content inside to layer it on top.

Low fidelity, high feelings.Move the pointer to look through the lens.
