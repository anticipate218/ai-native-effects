Effects / UI FX

ASCII Field

A flowing field of ASCII characters, rendered by a fragment shader. Each cell's brightness picks its glyph; the characters can gather into a word or an image, brighten under the pointer and ripple with a shock wave when you click. Put your own content inside to layer it on top.

Characters with somewhere to be.Move the pointer, click, or morph your own word.

Methods

MemberDescription
morph(text, ms?)Dissolves the current word and gathers the characters into a new one (Promise).
dissolve(ms?)Lets the word melt back into the field (Promise).
shockwave(x?, y?)Sends a wave from a point (element coordinates; default centre). Clicks do this too.
