Aurora Curtain

A hero background that feels like standing under the northern lights. Curtains of light hang from bright lower hems, rays rise and fade into violet, folds drift and lean towards your pointer, and stars twinkle above a mountain horizon. Wrap your hero in one tag; the content stays plain HTML.
