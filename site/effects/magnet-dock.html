Magnetic Dock

A dock for any buttons or links. Icons swell under the pointer on springs with a little overshoot, neighbours follow and lean in, one shared label glides between apps, and a launch bounces with squash and stretch. It is a real toolbar: arrow keys move focus and magnify, and on touch you can scrub along the dock.

Hover the dock, press Tab then arrow keys, or scrub with a finger. Click to launch.
