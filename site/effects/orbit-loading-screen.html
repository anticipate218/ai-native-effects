Orbit Loading Screen

A full loading screen for the long waits in AI and geo products. A WebGL planet is "downloaded" tile by tile: unloaded regions show a blurred preview, finished tiles snap into full detail while satellites sweep their swaths, and a typographic panel shows the percentage, steps and live details.

API

Attributes

AttributeValuesDefaultDescription
progress0–1 or 0%–100%noneProgress. Omit it while the total is unknown (indeterminate scan). Reaching 1 plays the completion flash.
headingstringPreparing your workspaceMain line. Changes fade in.
eyebrowstringLoadingSmall caps line above the heading, with a status beacon.
stepsA|B|CnonePipe-separated step labels.
stepinteger0Active step (0-based). Earlier steps show as done.
detailstringnoneSecondary line, e.g. sizes, speed and time left.
statusstringnoneMonospace log line (hidden on short screens).
errorbooleanoffFailed state: danger tone, the active step shows a cross, swaths stop.
satellites0–42Satellites sweeping the planet.
accentany CSS color#5ef2ffData color: fresh tiles, swaths, bar and step marks.
colorany CSS color#6aa6ffAtmosphere and preview tint.
fullscreenbooleanoffCovers the viewport (position: fixed).
sizeCSS length480pxHeight. The width follows the container; the layout stacks below 640px.
qualityauto high lowautoWebGL resolution. auto lowers it on slow devices.
speed0–51Animation time scale.
motionauto reduce fullautoauto follows prefers-reduced-motion.
labelstringheadingAccessible name of the progress bar.
seedinteger7Seed for the planet, orbits and stars.

Properties, slots and events

MemberDescription
progressnumber | null; null = indeterminate.
steps, step, error, satellitesReflect to their attributes (steps is a string array).
webglRead-only: false when the 2D fallback is in use.
slot="actions"Buttons under the panel, e.g. Cancel or Run in background.
ane-completeFired once each time progress reaches 100%. Bubbles and crosses shadow roots.
::part(screen), ::part(panel)Styling hooks.
